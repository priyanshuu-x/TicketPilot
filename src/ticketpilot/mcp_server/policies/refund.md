# Refund policy

- Delivered orders can be refunded within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- Orders that are not delivered yet cannot be refunded; follow the shipping policy instead.
- A refund can be partial, but the total refunded can never exceed the order amount.
- Refunds above **Rs 2000** need approval from a human reviewer.
- A customer's **second refund within 30 days** needs approval from a human reviewer.
- Always record the customer's reason for the refund.
